# The Story Behind Our Icon

**We come from the same roots. We are our own people. And we grow together.**

Our icon represents the balance between family, connection, and individuality.

The larger green figure represents love, care, and guidance. The smaller blue figure represents independence, identity, and finding your own way.

They meet at the bottom, symbolizing our shared roots and the connections that shape who we are. But the space between them is just as important. It represents privacy, boundaries, and the freedom to be ourselves.

Together, they form a heart, with a small sprout growing between them, representing health, healing, and the growth we experience individually and together.

Because **the strongest connections don't require us to lose ourselves. They give us the support and space to grow.**

*Carehold. Care that connects. Space to grow.*
